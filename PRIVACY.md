# Privacy Policy for CharCounter

**Last Updated:** August 2026  
**Publisher:** RMA Labs (Ricardo Daniel Masabel Avendaño)  
**Project:** [CharCounter](https://github.com/rmasabela/CharCounter/)

---

## 1. Overview
CharCounter is designed with complete privacy in mind. It is a local Windows desktop application for real-time text analysis.

## 2. Information Collection and Storage
- **No Personal Data:** CharCounter does not collect, log, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII).
- **Local Processing:** All text entered or pasted into the application is processed entirely in memory on your local device and is never sent over any network.
- **No Telemetry or Tracking:** The application does not contain analytics, telemetry trackers, background tracking agents, or advertising SDKs.

## 3. Network and Internet Access
CharCounter operates entirely offline and does not require an active internet connection to function. No outbound network connections are initiated by the application during runtime.

## 4. Third-Party Services
CharCounter does not share any data with third-party services or external servers.
